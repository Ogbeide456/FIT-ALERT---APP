# Fitalert User Agreement — Draft

**Draft for review; not legal advice.** This proposed agreement is not ready to bind users. Have qualified counsel adapt it to the operating entity, target countries, privacy practices, consumer-protection rules, and actual production services before launch.

## 1. Agreement and eligibility

By accessing Fitalert, you agree to these terms and any policies presented with the service. You must meet the minimum age required by the law where you live. If Fitalert is offered to minors, a separate age-appropriate design, parental-consent, and privacy review is required before that access is enabled.

## 2. Fitness and nutrition disclaimer

Fitalert provides general educational and wellness information only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, physical therapy, or a substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare professional. Exercise, diet, and use of equipment involve risks, including injury. You are responsible for choosing activities appropriate to your circumstances. Stop if you experience concerning symptoms and seek professional care when appropriate.

Plans, exercise demonstrations, AI responses, meal descriptions, and calorie estimates may be incomplete, inaccurate, unsuitable, or out of date. Do not rely on them for medical or dietary decisions. Consult qualified professionals for individualized advice.

## 3. Accounts and third-party authentication

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your account and for activity performed through it. Sign-in may be provided by Firebase Authentication and third-party providers such as Google, Microsoft, or Apple. Their terms and privacy notices apply to their services. Fitalert does not receive or store your provider password.

## 4. User information and privacy

You should provide accurate information and avoid submitting sensitive medical, financial, or identifying information in free-text fields or AI chats. Assessment answers, measurements, workout history, and meal entries may be processed as described in the applicable Privacy Notice. The current prototype keeps these entries in page state and does not provide account synchronization.

Before a production launch, Fitalert must publish a privacy notice explaining data collected, purposes, legal basis where applicable, retention, subprocessors, international transfers, user rights, and contact details.

## 5. Camera and microphone

Camera and microphone access is requested only after you choose to join a room. You can deny permission, disable either track, or leave. In this prototype, media is used for a local preview only and is not transmitted to another participant. If production group calling is later enabled, additional terms must disclose recipients, recording policy, retention, moderation, and any media provider.

## 6. AI and external content

AI-generated information is probabilistic and may be incorrect. Do not treat it as a medical professional or verified coach. When configured, prompts may be sent to OpenAI or Ollama, and search results may be provided by ExerciseDB or YouTube. Those providers operate independently under their own terms. Fitalert does not endorse all third-party content or verify all creator credentials.

## 7. Acceptable use and intellectual property

Do not misuse the service, attempt unauthorized access, upload unlawful content, infringe others’ rights, or interfere with other users. Fitalert and its original interface and materials are protected by applicable intellectual-property laws. Third-party catalogs, videos, creator names, and provider marks remain subject to their respective rights and licenses.

## 8. Availability and changes

Fitalert is provided on an “as available” basis to the extent allowed by law. Features may change, be suspended, or be removed. Nothing in this draft excludes consumer rights or liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.

## 9. Liability and governing law

Any limitation of liability, indemnity, dispute resolution, governing law, and venue provisions must be drafted by counsel for the actual operator and each market. Do not publish this draft as a final clause without that review.
